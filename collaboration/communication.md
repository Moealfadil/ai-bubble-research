# Team Communication Overview

This document summarizes how our team collaborates and communicates across platforms, meeting cadences, and norms that keep work moving efficiently.

## Communication Platforms

- WhatsApp: Fast, informal coordination for day-to-day updates, quick questions, and time-sensitive notifications.
- Google Meet: Primary video conferencing platform for scheduled discussions, screen shares, and working sessions.
- Zoom: Secondary conferencing option used when external participants prefer Zoom or for larger meetings requiring stable breakout features.

## Meeting Cadence

- Weekly sync: A standing weekly meeting to review progress, unblock tasks, and align on priorities for the next sprint.
- Issue-driven sessions: Ad hoc meetings scheduled when a specific problem, decision, or deep dive is needed (data issues, analysis design, code reviews).
- Milestone reviews: Short focused reviews tied to deliverables (reports, figures, notebook checkpoints) to confirm readiness and assign follow-ups.

## Working Norms

- Agenda-first: Meetings include a brief agenda circulated in advance; action items captured at the end with owners and due dates.
- Written-first updates: Status changes and decisions documented in the workspace (README, research_findings.md, or issue tracker) to maintain continuity.
- Responsiveness: WhatsApp for urgent matters; otherwise, replies within one business day on non-urgent topics.
- Screenshare as default: For technical discussions (notebooks, scripts, figures), we default to screen sharing to accelerate alignment.
